Open dsarsham-cmyk.github.io/autotrader/ on your phone. No PC or Telegram is needed to view this dashboard.

This is a read-only web dashboard, not a native phone app. It refreshes while open; it does not send background phone notifications. The trading engine runs separately in Railway.

Read the trend

Each ETF is checked using daily momentum and a volatility filter. Positive trend can permit a buy; weak trend can trigger an exit.

2

Size the risk

Position size adapts to volatility. Each portfolio has a configured 50% allocation cap. Market moves can change its current share. Lower configured risk does not guarantee smaller losses.

3

Manage the exit

Stop, take-profit and trailing rules decide when to leave. The audit verifies whether those protections actually exist at the broker.

The Windows app is your control center. The bot runs in Railway when this PC is off. Process uptime is not proof that orders or safeguards are working.

What “daily trading” really means

The engine uses daily market bars, but it is event-driven: it trades only when a signal or exit rule changes. It can hold positions for days and it cannot guarantee a fill or a profit every day. The activity calendar shows what actually happened.
